**Name:** Blocktide: Falling Blocks
**Subtitle:** Classic brick puzzle, no ads
**Keywords:** cube,drop,stack,tower,arcade,offline,brain,logic,casual,retro,neon,sprint,endless,kids,score,tiles
**Promotional Text:** New Sprint 40 race! Beat the clock, top the Game Center leaderboards, and chase a fresh high score. Three modes, three themes, no ads, no IAP. Free and fully offline.

**Description:**
Blocktide is a falling-blocks puzzle built for people who love the classic: the quiet satisfaction of steering a piece into place, clearing four lines at once, and watching the board settle back into calm. No gimmicks, no gacha, no pressure. Just you, the grid, and one more round.

Rotate, nudge, and drop seven familiar shapes as they fall faster and faster. Line up full rows to clear them, stack clears for bigger scores, and use hold and the next-piece preview to plan a move ahead. The second you spot the gap, hard-drop and go.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
Classic is an endless marathon that speeds up as you climb. Survive, stack cleanly, and push for a new personal best.
Sprint is a race to clear 40 lines as fast as you can. Pure focus, pure reflexes, one time to beat.
Endless is a relaxed, no-fail flow for when you just want to unwind with the blocks.

THREE THEMES
Neon wraps glowing pieces in deep dark space for late-night runs. Retro leans into warm, chunky pixels that nod to the handhelds you grew up with. Minimal Dark keeps everything quiet, high-contrast, and free of distraction.

BUILT TO FEEL GOOD
Smooth animation, tuned haptic feedback on every lock and line clear, a next-piece preview, hold, and instant hard drop. It sits comfortably in one hand on iPhone and stretches out beautifully on iPad.

GAME CENTER
Post your scores and Sprint times to global leaderboards, and unlock achievements as you sharpen your game. Sign-in is handled entirely by Apple.

HONEST BY DESIGN
No ads, ever. No in-app purchases. No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking. Blocktide plays fully offline, whether you are on a plane, on the subway, or off the grid. Available in English and Russian, on iPhone and iPad, for ages 4 and up.

Download Blocktide and clear a few lines. Then a few more.
