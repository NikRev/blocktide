## Description
BlockCraft is a polished Tetris experience built for iPhone and iPad.
Choose from Classic, Sprint, and Endless modes, customize the visual theme, and compete in Game Center leaderboards.

Features:
- Three gameplay modes for different play styles.
- Responsive controls with haptics and smooth animations.
- Theme customization (Neon, Retro, Minimal Dark).
- Game Center leaderboards and achievements.
- Session stats and personal progress tracking.
